SHD Loadout Bench

A build planner for The Division 2. Pick gear, assign attributes, see what the loadout totals. It calculates expected statistical numbers from gear, mods and set bonuses. It does not simulate damage.

Reporting anything? Include the version number shown at the top of the app, and a build code if it involves a specific loadout. Expanding a stat to see its sources usually narrows a problem down fast, and tells us whether it is bad data or bad maths. If something differs between two versions, say which two.

Known gaps in 0.6.0

These are missing or incomplete on purpose, not broken. Worth reading before reporting.
  • Weapons are deliberately incomplete. All 81 craftable weapons are selectable with base damage, rate of fire and magazine size. Only the 15 Assault Rifles have reload, range and attributes captured. A blank field means not captured, not zero.
  • No weapon damage output. A crafted weapon reads 38.6% above its base damage while its attributes account for only 32.6%. Until that is understood, showing a damage number would be a guess.
  • Weapon mods and talents are not in yet. Named weapon talents do not appear in the crafting preview at all, so they need a different source.
  • Skills, named and exotic gear are not in the tool yet.
  • Specializations are only partly built. The 45 point weapon pool works. The 120 point main tree does not — picking a specialization still adds almost nothing on its own. Every specialization also grants +50% Pulse Resistance that the tool does not yet show, so the hazard panel is understated.
  • Gear set attribute rolls are understated for 10 of 12 attributes. Only Crit Hit Chance and Crit Hit Damage have measured gear set values.
  • Ensnare and Shock resistance show n/a rather than 0. Those exist in game but have no captured values, and n/a means we do not know.
  • Gear set cores are locked for six sets. The other 21 let you pick freely, which may be wrong. If you know a set has a fixed core we have not locked, that is worth reporting.
  • China Light Industries shows no bonus values.
  • Hazard stacking between universal protection and a specific resistance has not been measured, so the combined total follows whichever model you pick. Treat it as a comparison aid, not a verified number.
